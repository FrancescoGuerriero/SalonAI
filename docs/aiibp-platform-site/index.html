Governed AI operating model

AI assists the business without becoming the business authority.

AIIBP keeps deterministic business state inside the module that owns it. AI assistants work across approved evidence and tools, with tenant isolation, permissions, provenance and human review where required.

Evidence groundedTenant isolatedPermission awareHuman supervisedAuditable

AI can

  • Summarise authorised business evidence
  • Detect anomalies and opportunities
  • Forecast and rank approved options
  • Recommend actions
  • Use explicitly approved tools
  • Explain recommendations with provenance

AI cannot

  • Cross tenant or location boundaries
  • Reveal credentials or secrets
  • Replace canonical business data
  • Bypass the module that owns an operation
  • Perform consequential actions without policy
  • Present generated output as deterministic evidence

Delivery model

From business requirements to an operational tenant.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Map the business model, locations, users, customer journeys, data, workflows and AI opportunities.

    Compose

    Select commercial modules and generate the dependency-closed installation plan.

  3. 03

    Configure

    Apply branding, terminology, RBAC, locations, integrations, data policies and module settings.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Run automated platform gates, tenant-isolation checks and role-based manual acceptance.

  5. 05

    Release

    Deploy through controlled release evidence with rollback readiness and explicit production activation.

  6. 06

    Evolve

    Add or remove modules, improve workflows and expand AI assistance without creating a tenant fork.
