About AIIBP

A modular SaaS foundation built around governed intelligence.

AIIBP is designed to deliver custom-made business platforms from a shared, upgradeable platform core. It separates commercial modules from internal platform engines so tenants receive a coherent product rather than a collection of duplicated features.

Platform architecture

Custom experience. Shared authority.

Commercial modules own business capabilities. Internal engines provide tenancy, identity, permissions, data, integrations, audit, workflows and AI governance. A tenant configuration determines what is enabled, not a forked application.

Composition model

AIIBP Core → Modules → Vertical eligibility → Business → Location → Roles & permissions → Integrations → AI policies → Tenant experience.

Core AI capabilities

AI supervises, assists and explains—within policy.

AI works across authorised module evidence to support people and workflows. Canonical operational state remains deterministic, permission-controlled and auditable.
